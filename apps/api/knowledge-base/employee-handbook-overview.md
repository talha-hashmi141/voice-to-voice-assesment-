# Employee Handbook Overview

## About Orbital Labs

Orbital Labs was founded in 2016 and builds **Orbit**, a satellite-data analytics platform used by agriculture, insurance and logistics customers. We are around 240 people across three offices: our headquarters in Austin (Texas), a European office in London, and an engineering hub in Karachi. Our mission is to turn raw Earth-observation data into decisions people can trust.

## Our values

- **Ground truth first.** We say what we know, we say what we do not know, and we never guess with a customer's money.
- **Small crews, big orbits.** Teams of four to eight people own outcomes end to end.
- **Leave it better.** Every document, pipeline and process should be clearer after you touched it.

## Working hours

Full-time employees work 40 hours per week on a flexible schedule. Everyone is expected to be reachable during the **core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 16:00 in their local office time zone**. Outside core hours you may arrange your day as you see fit, as long as your team agrees.

Wednesday afternoons are **meeting-free** company-wide so that people have uninterrupted time for deep work.

## Probation period

New employees have a **90-day introductory period**. During this time your manager will hold check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. Paid time off accrues from your first day but can be taken only after the first 30 days (see the PTO and Leave Policy).

## Payroll

Salaries are paid **semi-monthly, on the 15th and on the last business day of each month**. Payslips are available in OrbitHR, the HR portal. Expense reimbursements that have been approved are paid with the next payroll run.

## Communication norms

- Slack is our primary channel. During core hours, aim to respond to direct messages within **four hours**; it is fine to reply the next day outside of core hours.
- Decisions that affect more than one team must be written down in a decision record in Confluence.
- Email is reserved for external communication and official HR notices.
- Use your calendar to show your working hours; colleagues in other time zones rely on it.

## Dress code

There is no formal dress code. Dress for the people you will meet that day; customer visits usually call for business casual.

## Where to get help

- **People Operations (HR):** people@orbitallabs.example or the #people channel.
- **IT Helpdesk:** open a ticket in the helpdesk portal, ask the voice assistant to open one for you, or visit #it-help.
- **Finance:** finance@orbitallabs.example for payroll and expense questions.
