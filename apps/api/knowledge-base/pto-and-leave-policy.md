# PTO and Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Orbital Labs. Part-time employees receive PTO on a pro-rated basis.

## Paid time off (PTO) allowance

- Every full-time employee receives **20 days of paid time off per calendar year**.
- After **three years of continuous service** the allowance increases to **25 days per year**.
- PTO accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual allowance (1.67 days per month for a 20-day allowance). New hires accrue from their start date, so the first year is pro-rated.
- PTO can be taken only after the first **30 days of employment**.
- PTO is tracked in OrbitHR. Your current balance is always visible on your OrbitHR dashboard and through the voice assistant.

## Carryover of unused PTO

- You may carry **up to 5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the following year or they are forfeited.
- Any unused days above the 5-day limit are forfeited on 31 December. Orbital Labs does not pay out unused PTO except where local law requires it (for example on termination in the UK).

## Requesting PTO

- Submit requests in OrbitHR; your manager approves them.
- Requests for **three or more consecutive working days require at least two weeks' notice**.
- Requests for one or two days require at least **two business days' notice**.
- Company holidays that fall inside a requested period are **not deducted** from your PTO balance; only working days are counted.
- The Finance team has a blackout period during the **first week of every quarter** (quarter close) during which PTO is approved only in exceptional circumstances.
- If a request would take your balance below zero it will be rejected automatically. Negative balances are not allowed.

## Sick leave

- Employees receive **10 paid sick days per calendar year**. Sick days do not accrue; the full allowance is available on 1 January (pro-rated for new hires).
- Unused sick days **do not carry over** and are not paid out.
- A doctor's note is required for absences of **more than three consecutive working days**.
- Sick leave can also be used to care for an ill child, partner or parent.
- If you exhaust your sick leave, additional absence is taken as PTO, then unpaid leave.

## Parental leave

- **Primary caregivers** receive **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.
- **Secondary caregivers** receive **6 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.
- Leave can be taken any time within **12 months** of the birth or adoption and can be split into at most two blocks.
- Employees are eligible after **12 months of continuous service**. Employees with less service receive the statutory minimum for their country.
- Notify People Operations at least **8 weeks** before the intended start date where possible.

## Bereavement leave

- **5 paid days** for the death of an immediate family member (partner, child, parent, sibling).
- **2 paid days** for extended family (grandparents, in-laws, aunts, uncles).
- Additional time can be taken as PTO or unpaid leave with manager approval.

## Jury duty and civic leave

Orbital Labs pays your full salary for up to **10 working days** of jury duty or similar civic obligations per year. Provide the summons to People Operations.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to **30 days** per year may be granted with approval from your Vice President and People Operations. Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave.
