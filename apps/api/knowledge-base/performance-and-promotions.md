# Performance Reviews and Promotions

## Review cycles

Orbital Labs runs **two performance review cycles per year**:

- **April cycle**: reviews the period October to March.
- **October cycle**: reviews the period April to September.

Each cycle has three steps: self-review (one week), manager review and peer feedback (two weeks), and calibration across teams (one week). Results are shared in 1:1 meetings by the end of the cycle month.

## Ratings

Performance is rated on a four-point scale: **Developing, Meets expectations, Exceeds expectations, Exceptional**. Ratings consider results against goals and how the work was done, measured against our values.

## Levels

- Individual contributors: **L1 to L7**.
- Managers: **M1 to M4**.
- Level descriptions and expectations are published in the Career Framework in Confluence.

## Promotions

- Promotions are decided during the review cycles. Managers submit nominations in **March** (for the April cycle) and **September** (for the October cycle).
- A nomination requires a written promotion case with examples of sustained performance at the next level for at least **six months**, plus endorsements from two peers or stakeholders.
- Promotion decisions are made in calibration and approved by the department Vice President. Promotion to L6 and above requires approval from the executive team.
- Employees cannot be promoted during their 90-day introductory period.

## Compensation changes

- **Merit increases and promotion increases take effect on 1 July** (following the April cycle) and **1 January** (following the October cycle).
- Compensation bands for each level are reviewed yearly against market data. Band ranges are visible to managers in OrbitHR.
- Off-cycle adjustments are possible only to correct a clear market gap and need Vice President and People Operations approval.

## Performance improvement

If performance falls below expectations, the manager and People Operations agree a **performance improvement plan (PIP) of 60 days** with clear goals and weekly check-ins.
