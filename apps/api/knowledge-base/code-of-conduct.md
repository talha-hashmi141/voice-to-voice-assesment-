# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Orbital Labs does not tolerate discrimination, harassment, bullying or retaliation of any kind. This applies in the office, at remote work, at company events and in online channels.

## Harassment and discrimination

Harassment includes unwelcome comments or conduct based on race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability or any other protected characteristic. Anyone who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it (see below). All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially.

## Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept gifts or hospitality from customers, suppliers or partners worth **up to $100** per person per year. Anything above that must be declined or declared to Legal, who may approve it in writing.
- Never offer or accept anything that could be seen as a bribe or an attempt to influence a business decision.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose to your manager and People Operations any outside employment, board role or financial interest that could conflict with your work at Orbital Labs. Working for a direct competitor is not permitted while employed here.

## Confidentiality

Customer data, satellite imagery licences, product roadmaps and financial information are confidential. Share them only with people who need them for their work, and never post them on social media.

## Use of company resources

Company equipment, accounts and funds must be used for business purposes and in accordance with the IT Security Policy and the Expense Policy. Reasonable personal use of laptops and phones is allowed.

## Reporting concerns

- Talk to your manager, any People Operations partner, or email ethics@orbitallabs.example.
- Anonymous reports can be made through the independent **Speak Up line** linked from OrbitHR.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this Code.

## Consequences

Violations are handled proportionately, from coaching to dismissal, following the disciplinary process in the Employee Handbook.
