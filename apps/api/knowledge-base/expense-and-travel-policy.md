# Expense and Travel Policy

## Submitting expenses

- Submit all expense reports through **OrbitExpense** (the expense tool linked from OrbitHR).
- Expenses must be submitted **within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred**. Reports submitted later than 30 days are reimbursed only with written approval from your director and Finance.
- Approved reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run, normally within **10 business days** of approval.
- Expenses must be coded to a project or cost centre; your manager approves them.

## Receipts

- **A receipt is required for every single expense above $25.** For expenses of $25 or less, a receipt is recommended but not mandatory.
- Credit-card statements are not accepted as receipts; an itemised receipt is required.
- Photograph receipts with the OrbitExpense mobile app at the time of purchase whenever possible.

## Meals and per diem

- When travelling, meals are covered by a **per diem of $75 per day for domestic travel and $95 per day for international travel**. Per diem does not require receipts.
- On travel days, half the per diem applies if you depart after 14:00 or return before 12:00.
- Team meals in the office are reimbursed at up to **$30 per person**, with an itemised receipt.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## Flights

- Book flights through the corporate travel portal at least **14 days in advance** where possible.
- **Economy class** is the standard for flights under six hours. **Premium economy** is permitted for flights of six hours or longer; business class requires Vice President approval.
- Choose the lowest logical fare; a fare up to $100 more expensive may be chosen for a more convenient time.

## Hotels

- Standard hotel limit: **$250 per night**.
- High-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo): **$350 per night**.
- Extras such as minibar, movies and spa services are not reimbursable.

## Ground transport and mileage

- Rideshare and taxi fares are reimbursable with a receipt. Use public transport where practical.
- Driving your own car for business is reimbursed at **$0.70 per mile** (or the local statutory rate). Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursable.
- Rental cars: compact or intermediate class; purchase the rental company's basic insurance.

## Corporate card

- Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate credit card from Finance.
- Any single purchase above **$1,000** must be made on a corporate card or pre-approved by Finance.
- Personal purchases on the corporate card are prohibited.

## Non-reimbursable items

Parking fines, traffic tickets, personal entertainment, pet care, laundry on trips shorter than five days, and upgrades paid for personal comfort are not reimbursed.
