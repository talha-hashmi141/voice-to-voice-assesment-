# Benefits Overview

Benefits differ slightly by country. Details and plan documents are in the Benefits section of OrbitHR.

## Enrollment

- New employees must enroll in benefits **within 30 days of their start date**; otherwise coverage starts at the next open enrollment.
- **Open enrollment runs every November** for coverage starting 1 January.
- Life events such as marriage, birth or adoption allow changes within 30 days of the event.

## Health insurance (US)

- Medical plans: a **PPO** and an **HMO** through Blue Shield. Orbital Labs pays **85% of the employee premium and 60% of the premium for dependents**.
- Dental and vision plans are included with the company paying 100% of the employee premium.
- A Health Savings Account (HSA) is available with the high-deductible option; the company contributes **$1,000 per year**.

## Health insurance (UK)

- Private medical insurance through **Bupa** is provided for employees and can be extended to family members at the employee's cost.
- Dental and optical cash plans are available.

## Retirement

- **US:** 401(k) with a **100% company match on the first 4% of salary** you contribute. Matching contributions vest immediately.
- **UK:** workplace pension with a **5% employer contribution** when the employee contributes at least 3%.

## Learning and development

- Every employee has a **learning budget of $1,500 per year** for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
- Conference travel is covered separately under the Expense and Travel Policy with manager approval.
- Internal "Orbit Academy" courses are free and count toward the annual learning goal.

## Wellness

- A **$60 per month wellness allowance** for gym memberships, fitness apps or sports equipment, paid with payroll on submission of a receipt once per quarter.
- The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers **six free confidential counselling sessions** per year for employees and household members.
- Mental-health days can be taken from your sick leave allowance without a doctor's note.

## Life and disability insurance

- Life insurance at **two times annual salary** is provided at no cost.
- Short-term and long-term disability insurance covers 60% of salary after the waiting period.

## Other benefits

- Commuter benefit of **$100 per month** for public transport for office-based employees.
- Employee referral bonus of **$2,000** for hired referrals who pass probation.
- Volunteering: **two paid days per year** for volunteering with a registered charity.
