# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Orbital Labs! This guide explains what happens from your first day through the end of your 90-day introductory period.

## Before day one

- You will receive a welcome email with your start time, office address (or shipping confirmation for your laptop if you are remote) and a link to complete your personal details in OrbitHR.
- Remote employees receive their laptop by courier at least **two business days before their start date**.

## Day one

- IT sets up your laptop, accounts, password manager and MFA. Allow about two hours.
- Your manager introduces you to your **onboarding buddy**, a peer from another team who is your go-to person for informal questions during the first three months.
- You join the **Launch Pad** cohort: a group of new hires who start in the same month and attend onboarding sessions together.

## First week checklist

- Complete **security awareness training** (required within 30 days, but we recommend week one).
- Enroll in benefits in OrbitHR (**deadline: 30 days from your start date**).
- Submit your home-office stipend receipts if you are remote (deadline: 60 days).
- Read the Employee Handbook, the IT Security Policy and the Code of Conduct, and acknowledge them in OrbitHR.
- Set up your 1:1 schedule with your manager (weekly during probation).
- Attend the **Orbit platform training** (two half-day sessions) so you understand what we sell.

## 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins

Your manager holds structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. These cover your onboarding goals, what support you need and early feedback. The 90-day check-in concludes the introductory period.

## Introductory (probation) period

The introductory period lasts **90 days**. PTO accrues from day one but can be taken only after the first 30 days. During this period either party can end employment with one week's notice.

## Tools you will use

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| OrbitHR | HR portal: PTO, payslips, benefits, personal details |
| OrbitExpense | Expense reports and reimbursements |
| Slack | Day-to-day communication |
| Confluence | Documentation and decision records |
| Helpdesk portal | IT support tickets |
| 1Password | Password manager |

## Questions

Ask your buddy or post in #new-hires. People Operations runs an "Ask us anything" session for every Launch Pad cohort in week two.
