# Equipment and Laptop Policy

## Standard equipment

- Every employee is issued a company laptop on their first day: a **MacBook Pro 14-inch** by default, or a **Dell XPS 15** on request. Engineers may request a 16-inch MacBook Pro.
- Each employee is entitled to one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a noise-cancelling headset. Order accessories through the IT Helpdesk portal; standard items ship within **5 business days**.
- Laptops are refreshed **every three years**, or earlier if the device can no longer run the required software.

## Broken, damaged or malfunctioning devices

1. **Open an IT support ticket** describing the problem (or ask the voice assistant to open one). Use priority **High** if you cannot work at all, and **Medium** if you can still work with a workaround.
2. IT will triage within the Helpdesk SLA and arrange a repair or replacement.
3. If a repair takes longer than one business day, IT provides a **loaner laptop**: within **1 business day** in the Austin and London offices, and within **3 business days** by courier for remote employees.
4. Back up your work to the cloud drive continuously; IT cannot guarantee data recovery from a damaged disk.

Accidental damage (for example a cracked screen or spilled coffee) is covered by the company **once every two years** per employee without charge. Repeated accidental damage may be reviewed with your manager. Damage caused by intentional misuse may be charged to the employee.

## Lost or stolen devices

Follow the IT Security Policy: report within 24 hours with a P1 ticket so the device can be locked and wiped.

## Personal use

Reasonable personal use of your company laptop is allowed as long as it complies with the IT Security Policy and the Acceptable Use rules. Do not let family members or friends use company devices.

## Returning equipment

- When you leave Orbital Labs, return all company equipment **within 5 business days** of your last day. IT provides prepaid shipping labels for remote employees.
- Equipment that is not returned is invoiced at its depreciated value.
- Employees may buy their laptop at the depreciated price when it is replaced in the refresh cycle; ask IT for a quote.

## Mobile phones

Orbital Labs does not provide mobile phones except for on-call roles. On-call engineers and account managers receive a company phone or a **$50 monthly phone allowance** (this is the same allowance described in the Remote Work Policy and is not paid twice).
