# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule

- Office-based roles in Austin and London follow a **hybrid schedule with three anchor days in the office: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday**. Monday and Friday are flexible.
- Teams may agree different anchor days with their director; the agreement must be recorded in the team's charter.
- **Fully remote roles** are approved per role by the hiring Vice President. Existing employees may apply to become fully remote after 12 months of service.

## Core hours and availability

Regardless of location, employees must be available during the **core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 16:00 local time**. Keep your Slack status and calendar up to date so colleagues know when you are working.

## Working from another country

- Employees may work from a country other than their employment country for **up to 30 calendar days per calendar year**.
- Submit a "Work from abroad" request in OrbitHR at least **two weeks in advance**. Requests need approval from your manager and from the Security team, because some countries are restricted for data-protection and export-control reasons.
- You must use the company VPN at all times when working abroad and must not take customer data on physical media.
- Trips longer than 30 days require a formal relocation review with People Operations and Legal; approval is not guaranteed.

## Home office stipend

- New employees receive a **one-time home office stipend of $500** (or the local equivalent) to buy a desk, chair or monitor. Submit the receipts through the expense tool within 60 days of your start date.
- All employees receive a **$50 per month internet and phone allowance**, paid automatically with payroll; no receipts are needed.
- Company-owned equipment (laptop, headset, monitor) is provided by IT; see the Equipment Policy.

## Remote work expectations

- Work from a secure location: no shared or public screens for customer data, and lock your screen when you step away.
- Use a headset and a quiet space for customer calls.
- Co-working space memberships are reimbursable up to **$200 per month** for fully remote employees with manager approval.

## Time zones

When teams span more than one office, meetings should be scheduled inside the overlapping core hours. Recurring meetings outside someone's core hours need that person's agreement, and recordings or notes must be shared for anyone who cannot attend.
