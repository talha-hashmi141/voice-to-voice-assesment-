# IT Helpdesk and Support Process

## How to get support

You can open a support ticket in three ways:

1. Through the **Helpdesk portal** (helpdesk.orbitallabs.example).
2. By asking the **voice assistant** to open a ticket for you; it will confirm the title, description and priority before creating it.
3. By posting in **#it-help** on Slack; an agent will convert the thread into a ticket.

Every ticket receives a reference number in the form **TCK-XXXXXX**. Quote it in any follow-up.

## Priority levels and service targets

| Priority          | When to use it                                                                                                          | First response target | Resolution target |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------- |
| **P1 – Critical** | You cannot work at all, a security incident, a lost or stolen device, or a customer-facing outage                       | 1 hour                | 4 hours           |
| **P2 – High**     | A major function is broken and there is no workaround (for example your laptop will not boot)                           | 4 hours               | 1 business day    |
| **P3 – Medium**   | Something is broken but you have a workaround (for example a cracked screen, a faulty monitor, software not installing) | 1 business day        | 3 business days   |
| **P4 – Low**      | Requests and questions: accessories, new software, how-to questions                                                     | 2 business days       | 5 business days   |

Targets are measured during Helpdesk hours. The ticket priority can be changed by the Helpdesk agent after triage; you will be told why.

## Helpdesk hours

- The Helpdesk operates **24 hours a day, Monday to Friday**, following the sun across Karachi, London and Austin.
- At weekends an on-call engineer handles **P1 tickets only**. Lower-priority tickets opened at the weekend are picked up on Monday.

## Ticket life cycle

Tickets move through the statuses **Open → In progress → Resolved**. You will receive an email and a Slack notification at each change. A resolved ticket is closed automatically after five days if you do not reply.

## Escalation

If a ticket has breached its response target, reply to the ticket with "escalate" or message the IT Manager in #it-help. Repeated breaches are reviewed in the monthly service review.

## What the Helpdesk does not handle

- HR questions (PTO balances, payroll): contact People Operations.
- Expense questions: contact Finance.
- Building and facilities issues: use the Facilities form in the portal.
