# IT Security Policy

Security is part of everyone's job. Violations of this policy are handled under the Code of Conduct.

## Accounts and passwords

- Passwords must be **at least 14 characters** long and unique for every service. Use the company password manager (1Password), which is provided free to every employee.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory** on all company accounts. Use the authenticator app; SMS codes are allowed only as a backup.
- Never share your credentials, even with IT. The IT Helpdesk will never ask for your password.
- Accounts that are unused for 90 days are disabled automatically.

## Devices and encryption

- Only company-managed laptops may be used to access production systems and customer data.
- Full-disk encryption (FileVault or BitLocker) is enforced by device management and must not be disabled.
- Screens must lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
- USB mass-storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Use the approved cloud drive to move files.
- Software must be installed from the Self Service catalogue; requests for other software go through an IT ticket and a security review.

## Network and VPN

- Connect through the **company VPN whenever you use a public or untrusted Wi-Fi network** (hotels, airports, cafés) and whenever you work from abroad.
- Do not connect company devices to networks that require you to disable the VPN or install a certificate.

## Phishing and suspicious messages

- Report suspicious emails with the **"Report Phish" button** in your mail client or by forwarding them to security@orbitallabs.example. Reporting takes priority over deleting.
- Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders. When in doubt, ask in #security.
- The Security team runs phishing simulations; there is no penalty for clicking a simulated link, but you will be asked to retake the training.

## Lost or stolen devices

- **Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 24 hours** by opening a **P1 ticket** with the IT Helpdesk or calling the security hotline. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.
- Change your passwords for any account that was signed in on the device.
- Replacement devices are issued under the Equipment Policy; there is no charge to the employee for a first loss.

## Personal devices

- Company email and Slack may be used on personal phones only through the managed apps (Microsoft Intune). Customer data must never be stored on a personal device.
- Personal laptops may not be used for work.

## Security training

- All employees complete security awareness training **within their first 30 days** and **annually** thereafter.
- Engineers with production access complete additional secure-coding training every year.

## Incident reporting

If you believe a security incident has occurred (for example data sent to the wrong recipient), report it immediately to security@orbitallabs.example. Early reporting is never punished; concealment is.
