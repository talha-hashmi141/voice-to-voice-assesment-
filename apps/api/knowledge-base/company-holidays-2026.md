# Company Holidays 2026

Company holidays are paid days off and are **not deducted from your PTO balance**. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday is observed. Each office follows its own list below.

## Austin (US) office holidays 2026

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January 2026 | Thursday | New Year's Day |
| 19 January 2026 | Monday | Martin Luther King Jr. Day |
| 25 May 2026 | Monday | Memorial Day |
| 19 June 2026 | Friday | Juneteenth |
| 3 July 2026 | Friday | Independence Day (observed, 4 July is a Saturday) |
| 7 September 2026 | Monday | Labor Day |
| 26 November 2026 | Thursday | Thanksgiving Day |
| 27 November 2026 | Friday | Day after Thanksgiving |
| 24 December 2026 | Thursday | Christmas Eve |
| 25 December 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day |
| 31 December 2026 | Thursday | New Year's Eve |

That is **11 paid holidays** for the Austin office in 2026. In addition, 1 January 2027 (Friday) is a holiday under the 2027 calendar.

The London office observes the **8 UK bank holidays** plus the company-wide closure on **24 December and 31 December** (10 days in total).

## Year-end closure

The offices in Austin and London close early at 13:00 on 23 December. 24, 25 and 31 December are company holidays in both offices. **28, 29 and 30 December 2026 are normal working days**; if you want the whole last week of December off, request PTO for those days.

## Floating holiday

Every employee also receives **one floating holiday per year** to use for a religious or cultural observance of their choice. Request it in OrbitHR like a normal PTO day; it is not deducted from the PTO balance and cannot be carried over.

## Karachi office

The Karachi office follows the public-holiday calendar published separately by People Operations each January.
